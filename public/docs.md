## Getting Started

### What is Turbine?

Turbine is a fully integrated **business operating system** that unifies finance, sales, supply chain, HR, marketing, services, and productivity apps in one platform. It is hosted on AWS and managed end-to-end so your team focuses on operations — not infrastructure.

**Tagline:** Your Business deserves an Operating System.

### Who it is for

- Growing companies replacing spreadsheets and disconnected tools
- Manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and service businesses
- Teams that want one system for CRM, inventory, accounting, and people ops

## Implementation Guide

### Typical journey

1. **Discovery** — Processes, systems, and success criteria.
2. **Blueprint** — App selection, data model, and integration needs.
3. **Configuration** — Company setup, workflows, roles, and Studio adjustments.
4. **Data migration** — Import customers, products, opening balances as agreed.
5. **Training** — Role-based sessions so adoption sticks.
6. **Go-live & hypercare** — Stabilization with active support.

### Methodology notes

- **Smaller teams** — Faster standard approach with core apps first.
- **Larger / complex orgs** — Phased rollout (often 80/20: standardize first, customize where it matters).
- **Partners / multi-site** — Local process alignment with multi-company where needed.

### Best practices

- Start with the processes that hurt the most (e.g. quoting + inventory + invoicing).
- Nominate internal champions per department.
- Keep customizations intentional — prefer configuration over one-off code when possible.
- Review dashboards weekly in the first month after go-live.

## FAQ

### How does one-time + monthly pricing work?

You pay a one-time implementation fee to configure Turbine for your business, then a transparent per-user monthly subscription covering hosting, updates, support, and app access.

### Are all apps included?

On Growth and Enterprise plans, every app in the Turbine suite is available. Starter focuses on a curated set of core apps — you can upgrade anytime.

### Who manages the infrastructure?

We do. Turbine runs on AWS under our control — provisioning, security, upgrades, and reliability are handled for you.

### Can we customize workflows?

Yes. Use Studio to automate actions, design screens, build reports, and adapt processes without starting from scratch.

### How do we get support?

Support is included with your subscription. Growth and Enterprise plans include higher priority and, where applicable, a dedicated success contact.

### Where do we start?

Visit the Contact page and request a demo. Bring a short list of your current tools and the three processes you most want to improve.
